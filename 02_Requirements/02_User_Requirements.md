## Core user needs (summary)

- Clients: book and attend consultations, purchase products, manage owned animals.
- Employees: clinical work (vets), reception and stock (assistants), HR (schedules, absences, clock-in).
- Administrators: roles and permissions (RBAC), account lifecycle.
- External partners: shelters and suppliers linked to intake and purchases.
