# Business Requirements

## Objective
Describe the business goals and project rules that guide implementation decisions.

## Project Goals
- Provide a reliable platform for managing project data
- Ensure information integrity and traceability
- Support decision-making with consistent data
- Ensure compliance with academic and regulatory requirements

## Business Rules
- Each user must have a unique profile
- Critical data cannot be deleted without proper authorization
- Record changes must be auditable
- All entries must comply with defined domain validations

## Legal and Regulatory Constraints
- Protect personal data according to applicable laws
- Use intellectual property and image rights appropriately
- Comply with the project’s academic standards
