## Two intentional layers

| Layer | Folder | Question |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Narrative Stories** | [01_Narrative_Stories/](01_Narrative_Stories/) | *Who are they, and how do their paths intersect?* |
| **Operational Scenarios** | [02_Operational_Scenarios/](02_Operational_Scenarios/) | *What exactly happened, and what must the system enforce?* |

**Narrative** = human-centered prose for onboarding, demos, and academic context.  
**Operational** = validation contract for DemoData, QA, and workflow states (may reference services and column semantics by design).

If the two disagree, trust **`02_Operational_Scenarios/`**.
