# Associação Patinhas do Ave

Associação Patinhas do Ave is a **partner shelter** in the Braga launch ecosystem. Animals move between the association and MiaCaoMigo’s internal shelter corridor under Tiago Mendes’s management.

## Role in the network

Patinhas is the external organisation behind **Rogerim**’s intake on **4 May** and Gonçalo Rego’s adoption on **8 May**. The same partner thread includes **Max** returning on **20 May** — a concession-style movement that shows the association is not only a one-way supplier of dogs.

The operational material positions Patinhas as the named shelter partner for **transfer in** and **return out**, distinct from Resgate Minhoto’s rescue identity and from NorteVet’s commercial supply role.

## Interaction rhythm

Early May concentrates **incoming** partner animals that become internal shelter stock and then client adoptions. Mid-May adds **outgoing** movement for Max, balancing Rogerim’s successful placement narrative.

Patinhas does not appear as Marta’s retail supplier or as the source for Quico’s abandonment intake in the documented events.

## People on the clinic side

Tiago Mendes is the consistent operational face. Gonçalo is the adopting client for Rogerim. Ivo and desk staff sit in the background as administrators and coordinators; João Marcelo’s work begins after animals attach to clients.

Patinhas makes the clinic legible as **embedded in a local welfare network**, not as an isolated practice.
