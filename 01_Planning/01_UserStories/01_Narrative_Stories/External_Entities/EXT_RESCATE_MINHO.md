# Resgate Minhoto

Resgate Minhoto is the launch simulation’s **emergency rescue partner** — distinct from Associação Patinhas do Ave’s shelter transfers and from NorteVet’s wholesale supply role.

## Role in the network

Based in the Guimarães periphery, Resgate Minhoto handles **short-notice intakes** when animals are found in distress rather than handed over through a standing shelter pipeline. Volunteers coordinate with the clinic at the loading bay; delivery witnesses in the operational material often include Tiago Mendes and sometimes João Marcelo in an off-clinical assist role.

The dated anchor for this partner is **4 May 2026**, when Resgate Minhoto delivers **Quico** — a dog abandoned near a highway rest area. Tiago registers Quico and records the delivery; Quico remains **internal**, in kennel B-12 in the operational notes, awaiting adoption through the end of the May–June window.

## Distinction from Patinhas

Patinhas supplies Rogerim and Pipoca through transfer deliveries that precede client adoptions. Resgate Minhoto supplies **rescue urgency** and abandonment origin language without placing Quico into Gonçalo’s or Ana’s households in the documented chronology.

## Clinic relationships

Tiago is the consistent handler for intake. Marcelo appears at the margins of delivery witness lists. No retail or invoice arc runs through Resgate Minhoto — Navarro and client purchases sit on a different plane.

Resgate Minhoto makes the external cast **three-shaped**: shelter partner, rescue partner, supplier.
