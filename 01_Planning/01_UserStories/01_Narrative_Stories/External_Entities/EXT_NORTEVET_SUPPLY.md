NorteVet Supply Lda is the clinic’s **wholesale supplier** in the launch simulation — the commercial back office counterpart to Marta Ribeiro’s retail purchases at the front desk.

## Role in the network

On **19 May 2026**, João Navarro’s timeline includes a **purchase order** event with NorteVet. That single dated anchor ties the ecosystem to **inbound stock** from outside without describing product catalogs or inventory tables in this layer.

NorteVet does not adopt animals, receive shelter returns, or appear in client waiting rooms. It exists so the clinic is modeled as both **care site** and **stocked business**.
