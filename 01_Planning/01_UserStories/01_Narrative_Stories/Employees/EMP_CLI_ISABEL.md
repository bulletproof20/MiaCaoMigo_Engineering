# Isabel Carvalho

Isabel Carvalho is the clinic’s **hybrid figure**: she coordinates the front desk after an early-May absence, and she is also a **client** with her own cats. That dual presence is deliberate in the launch simulation — the same person can greet Gonçalo and later sit in the waiting room logic as an animal owner.

## Role as employee

From **5–20 May**, Isabel is in **detected absence** — not yet at the desk in the launch-week sense. Tiago and the absence layer document those gaps as staffing signals. On **6 May** she still helps **Ana Lourenço** by phone to request Pipoca’s June booking before Ana’s adoption completes. From **21 May** onward she is the active front desk; Gonçalo’s informal early contact fits that bridge period.

She does not own shelter transfers, supplier orders, or administrator configuration — Ivo, Tiago, and Navarro hold those edges.

## Role as client

As a client, Isabel keeps **Tico**, **Teco**, and **Jerónimo** in the household cast. **22 May** is Tico’s chain: scheduling through clinical completion and invoicing with João Marcelo. A **21 May** morning reminder targets Tico as a client notification. Teco and Jerónimo are named cohabiting cats without separate dated visit arcs in the provided material.

A **3 June** **pending** absence appears on the employee side after the main launch window — another staffing beat without inventing new clinical events.

Isabel’s client animals are **not** duplicated as standalone files in the Animals operational folder; only Tico’s dated clinical event is spelled out in depth. Narratives for Teco and Jerónimo therefore remain implicit inside Isabel’s story rather than as invented adventures.

## Why she matters to the ecosystem

Isabel proves that **employee and client boundaries** can coexist in one real organisation: desk coordination, public support scenarios, and “my cat’s appointment” can reference the same person without merging accounts incorrectly in the reader’s mind — the operational layer keeps the rules; this layer keeps the human picture.

## Relationships that matter

João Marcelo is Tico’s clinician on **22 May**. Gonçalo is an early informal contact. Tiago appears in absence detection context. Other clients — Marta, Ana, Pedro — do not centre on Isabel in the documented events.
