# Ivo Sá

Ivo Sá is the **platform administrator** for MiaCaoMigo Braga in the launch simulation. He is present from the first day of the operational window and owns the configuration and account-creation work that lets the rest of the cast appear on the timeline.

## Role in the ecosystem

As administrator, Ivo sits above the day-to-day shelter, clinical, and commercial flows. He is not the narrative centre of adoption or consultation stories, but he is the person who makes those stories possible in the system: client registrations for Gonçalo, Marta, Ana, and Pedro fall within the onboarding burst he supports, and employee accounts for Tiago, Navarro, Marcelo, and Isabel depend on the same administrative layer.

His work aligns with **1 May 2026** as the simulation anchor — clinic go-live, team presence, and the start of chronology entries that reference administrator actions.

## How he interacts with others

Tiago Mendes depends on administrative stability to record shelter intakes and adoptions. João Navarro and João Marcelo need roles, schedules, and permissions consistent with commercial desk and veterinary lead duties. Isabel Carvalho’s hybrid identity — employee and client — also passes through the administrative boundary Ivo represents.

When Gonçalo Rego later generates failed sign-in attempts, the audit flavour of those events is administrator-relevant even though Ivo is not portrayed as personally resetting passwords in the narrative material.

## What his story is not

The operational layer does not assign Ivo a heavy clinical calendar, shelter transfers, or retail returns. His narrative is **enabling infrastructure**: the clinic’s digital twin stays coherent while front-of-house stories play out.
