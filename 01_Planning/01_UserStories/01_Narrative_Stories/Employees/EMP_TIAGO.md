# Tiago Mendes

Tiago Mendes is the clinic’s **animal and shelter manager** in the launch window. His work connects external partner intake, internal shelter animals, adoptions, and one concession back to a partner organisation.

## Role in the ecosystem

From **4 May** onward, Tiago is the operational face of “animal arrives from outside” and “animal leaves for adoption or return.” He receives **Rogerim** from Associação Patinhas do Ave and later completes Gonçalo Rego’s adoption. He receives **Pipoca** before Ana Lourenço’s adoption. He handles **Quico** as an abandonment intake who remains internally awaiting a home. On **20 May**, he processes **Max** moving back to Patinhas under a concession pattern documented in the external partner story.

Tiago also appears in **absence detection** entries tied to Isabel Carvalho’s early May gap — the chronology records detected absences before Isabel returns to desk coordination. That linkage is operational, not a personal conflict narrative; it shows how staffing visibility intersects shelter work.

## Rhythm of the first weeks

The first week clusters partner transfers and internal status: Rogerim and Pipoca establish the shelter corridor; Quico adds a non-partner abandonment path; Max closes the loop with Patinhas on return. Adoptions to Gonçalo and Ana land on **8** and **9 May**, giving Tiago back-to-back “animal becomes family member” completions.

Tiago does not own Felix’s clinical arc, Marta’s returns, or Pedro’s no-show. His story stays on the **animal lifecycle** side of the house.

## Relationships that matter

Associação Patinhas do Ave is his most frequent external counterpart. Gonçalo and Ana are the adopting clients for Rogerim and Pipoca. João Marcelo and João Navarro take over after animals attach to clients or when clinical and commercial events supersede intake.

Tiago’s narrative is the bridge between **external entities** and **household animals** inside Braga operations.
