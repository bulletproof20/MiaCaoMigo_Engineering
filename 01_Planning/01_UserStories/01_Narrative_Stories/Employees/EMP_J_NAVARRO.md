# João Navarro

João Navarro is the **commercial desk** lead in the launch simulation. Purchases, returns, supplier-facing purchase orders, and the invoice side of client relationships run through his timeline more than through any other employee.

## Role in the ecosystem

Navarro registers commercially significant moments for **Marta Ribeiro** — a purchase on **10 May** and a return on **18 May** — and supports **Gonçalo Rego** when retail aligns with Jonas’s nutrition change after dermatology. He is the natural counterpart when the ecosystem needs **stock movement tied to clients** rather than shelter intake.

On **19 May**, his work extends to **NorteVet Supply Lda** with a purchase order event, linking the clinic’s back office to an external supplier without turning the narrative into inventory schema language.

## How he differs from clinical staff

João Marcelo owns consultation completion, lateness, cancellations, and clinical load including the **11 May** double-booking stress day. Navarro stays on the **money and goods** plane: what was sold, what came back, what was ordered from a wholesaler, and which invoice is overdue for Marta by **5 June**.

He does not adopt animals or receive shelter transfers. Tiago Mendes hands off to Navarro once animals belong to clients who shop.

## Relationships that matter

Marta is his friction client; Gonçalo is his repeat buyer; Marta’s overdue invoice closes the commercial arc into June. NorteVet is the supplier partner named in his chronology. Ivo Sá remains the administrator backdrop.

Navarro’s story makes the clinic feel like a **business** as well as a care site — returns and payables sit alongside Felix’s visits in the same calendar.
