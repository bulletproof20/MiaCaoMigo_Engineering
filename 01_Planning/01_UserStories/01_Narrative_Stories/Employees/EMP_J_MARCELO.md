# João Marcelo

João Marcelo is the **veterinary lead** for the Braga launch window. Consultations, follow-ups, specialty visits, end-of-life recording, and scheduling pressure on his calendar define his place in the ecosystem.

## Role in the ecosystem

Marcelo completes or owns the clinical threads for **Felix** (Marta Ribeiro’s dog), **Jonas** (Gonçalo Rego’s cat across general and dermatology), **Bento** (Ana Lourenço’s senior cat through to deceased status on **28 May**), and **Tico** (Isabel Carvalho’s cat as client animal on **22 May** with prescription follow-through in the operational chain). He is the named clinician when the timeline needs **completed visits**, **late arrival** still completed, or **future scheduled** visits tied to medical specialties.

**11 May** is his stress day in the chronology: a **double-booking** event exercises calendar conflict handling without inventing a second veterinarian. The narrative treats that day as operational load, not personal drama.

## Clinical rhythm

Felix’s path — **10 May** consultation and **17 May** follow-up after Marta arrives late — anchors the “busy dog owner” clinical side. Jonas’s **12 May** general and **16 May** dermatology visits link directly to Gonçalo’s retail follow-up. Bento’s decline gives the ecosystem a **deceased animal** signal tied to real care rather than admin deletion. Tico’s **22 May** visit connects the hybrid Isabel storyline back to Marcelo as vet, not only as desk coordinator.

Marcelo does not process shelter intakes, Patinhas transfers, or NorteVet purchase orders. Tiago and Navarro own those planes.

## Relationships that matter

Gonçalo, Marta, Ana, and Isabel-as-client are his household-facing clients in the cast. Pedro Costa’s no-show implies a scheduled Marcelo slot but the operational animal files centre on Thor only through Pedro’s customer record. Isabel as employee re-enters after absence detection; Marcelo’s calendar is independent of her desk return except where shared days collide.

Marcelo’s narrative is the **medical credibility** of the clinic during the first six weeks — including one deliberate scheduling failure mode on 11 May.
