# Bento

Bento is **Ana Lourenço’s senior cat** and the launch simulation’s clearest **end-of-life** animal narrative.

## Place in the household

Bento shares Ana’s home with **Pipoca**, the cat adopted from shelter intake days earlier. While Pipoca represents new family growth, Bento carries the weight of older-animal care and closure inside the same May window.

## Care and outcome

João Marcelo’s clinical involvement for the Lourenço household includes Bento in the mid-May veterinary load described in the ecosystem cast. On **28 May 2026**, Bento is recorded as **deceased** in the operational material. No additional invented ceremonies or diagnoses appear here — the fact is simply that the clinic’s records reflect death as a status change tied to real care history.

Pipoca’s **12 June** scheduled visit remains on the calendar afterward, which is narratively important: the household continues with the surviving cat.

## Ecosystem role

Bento exists so documentation can discuss **bereavement and animal status** in human terms, distinct from Pedro’s inactive client account or Quico’s indefinite internal waiting. Bento does not interact with external shelters or commercial returns.

## Relationships

Ana is the owning client. Marcelo is the clinician context. Tiago’s shelter work applies to Pipoca, not Bento’s arc.
