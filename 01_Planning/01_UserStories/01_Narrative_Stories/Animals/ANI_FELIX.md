# Felix

Felix is **Marta Ribeiro’s dog** and the clinical anchor for a client who also generates commercial and scheduling friction.

## Care over time

Felix is **adopted on 8 May** from a local shelter mix, not from Patinhas. João Marcelo handles an acute limp across **13–14 May** (in progress, then completed with delay). A **follow-up** on **17 May** completes after Marta arrives **late** following traffic on the A3. A surgery-prep slot is **cancelled on 24 May** for a June date.

Felix does not adopt from shelter stock, does not die in the window, and does not transfer to external partners. His medical path stays comparatively straightforward compared to Jonas’s multi-specialty arc or Bento’s end of life.

## Ambient contact

On **17 May**, Felix’s appointment shares waiting-room chronology with Gonçalo Rego’s presence — two households in the same clinic moment without merging stories.

## Ecosystem role

Felix lets readers connect **Marta’s clinical normalcy** to **her commercial noise** (return **18 May**, cancellation **24 May**, overdue invoice **5 June**). The dog is the reason Marta keeps coming back medically even when retail and accounts receivable look uneven.

## Relationships

Marta is the client. Marcelo is the veterinarian. Navarro is the commercial counterpart for purchases and returns tied to the same household week.
