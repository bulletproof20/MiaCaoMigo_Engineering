# Rogerim

Rogerim is a **shelter-to-adoption dog** whose path defines the partner-intake story for Gonçalo Rego’s household.

## Origin and movement

On **4 May 2026**, Tiago Mendes receives Rogerim from **Associação Patinhas do Ave** into the clinic’s internal shelter corridor. For four days Rogerim remains an internal animal awaiting a client link. On **8 May**, Tiago completes the adoption that assigns Rogerim to **Gonçalo Rego**, who had registered two days earlier.

That short gap is narratively important: it mirrors realistic sequencing between partner transfer, client onboarding, and adoption completion without implying a failed placement.

## Life after adoption

Once with Gonçalo, Rogerim is part of the anchor household narrative alongside **Jonas** the cat. The operational animal file centres on intake and adoption; Rogerim does not carry the multi-visit clinical density Jonas receives in May and June. Rogerim’s role in the ecosystem is **successful partner shelter placement**, not chronic specialty care.

## Partners and people

Patinhas do Ave is the external source. Tiago is the intake and adoption actor. Gonçalo is the owning client. João Marcelo and João Navarro serve the household indirectly through Jonas’s visits and purchases rather than through a long Rogerim-only clinical list in the provided material.
