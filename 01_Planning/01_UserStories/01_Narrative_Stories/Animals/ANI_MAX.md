# Max

Max is the **return-to-partner** animal in the launch window — movement back to Associação Patinhas do Ave under Tiago Mendes’s shelter management.

## Movement on 20 May

On **20 May 2026**, Tiago processes Max leaving client-facing placement and returning to Patinhas in the **concession** pattern described in the operational external-entity material. Max is not adopted by Gonçalo or Ana in the documented chronology; the narrative is closure of a partner relationship, not a new household.

## Contrast with other animals

Rogerim and Pipoca travel **into** the clinic then **out** to families. Quico stays **internal** without partner return. Max travels **back** to the association, exercising the ecosystem’s need to show animals that do not only flow one direction.

## Ecosystem role

Max supports partner shelter realism: associations both **supply** animals and **receive** them again when placement models require it. No commercial desk or Marta-style invoice story attaches to Max in the provided files.

## Relationships

Tiago is the operational actor. Patinhas do Ave is the external counterpart. Active clients in the cast do not own Max in the May–June events captured here.
