# Quico

Quico is an **abandonment intake** who remains in **internal** shelter status through the end of the documented simulation — an animal still awaiting a home.

## How Quico arrives

On **4 May 2026**, **Resgate Minhoto** delivers Quico to the clinic loading bay — an abandonment case from a highway rest area, not an owner surrender. Tiago Mendes registers the animal and records the delivery; volunteers and clinic staff witness the handoff in the operational story. Quico widens the corridor beyond Patinhas-style shelter transfers while staying distinct from Gonçalo’s or Ana’s adoption paths.

## What does not happen in the window

Quico does not complete adoption to Gonçalo, Ana, or Pedro within May–June 2026. Quico does not generate the clinical density of Jonas or Felix, or the return-and-invoice story of Marta. The operational point is **waiting**: internal animal, future placement still open.

## Ecosystem role

Quico balances the success stories of Rogerim and Pipoca. Readers see a clinic that can hold animals **in limbo** as well as move them to families or back to partners (as with Max).

## Relationships

Tiago is the intake actor. Resgate Minhoto is the rescue partner. No owning client is attached in the launch cast.
