# Pipoca

Pipoca is **Ana Lourenço’s adopted cat**, received through the same shelter corridor as Rogerim but on a parallel calendar.

## Origin and movement

Tiago Mendes records Pipoca’s reception on **5 May**, one day after Rogerim’s Patinhas intake. Pipoca stays internal until **9 May**, when Ana completes adoption — the day after Gonçalo adopts Rogerim. The two adoptions back-to-back show the clinic handling multiple partner and internal placements in the same week.

## Life after adoption

Pipoca survives the household’s other major event: **Bento**’s death on **28 May**. Pipoca’s forward path includes a **scheduled** visit on **12 June**, placing the surviving cat still inside active care planning while Ana processes loss.

Pipoca does not appear in Marta’s retail friction, Pedro’s no-show, or Max’s return to Patinhas.

## Partners and people

Tiago owns intake and adoption. Ana is the client. João Marcelo is the clinician for the household; Bento carried the heavier mid-May clinical weight until deceased status. Pipoca represents **continuity** — adoption plus future wellness scheduling after bereavement.
