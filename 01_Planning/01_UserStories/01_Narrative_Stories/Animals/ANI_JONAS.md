# Jonas

Jonas is **Gonçalo Rego’s cat** and the clinical plus retail driver for the busiest household in the launch window.

## Place in the household

Unlike Rogerim, who arrives through Patinhas, Jonas is already Gonçalo’s animal when the May simulation begins. That makes Jonas the reference pet for **repeat veterinary care** and for **shop behaviour tied to treatment** — food and toys on **10 May**, nutrition linked to dermatology on **18 May**.

## Care over time

João Marcelo completes a **general consultation** on **12 May** and **dermatology** on **16 May**, the latter influencing diet decisions that Navarro’s desk reflects commercially two days later. A **dermatology appointment remains scheduled** for **10 June**, with reminder activity on **9 June** in the notification rhythm shared with other future visits.

Jonas does not pass through shelter internal status in the documented arc. Quico, Pipoca, and Rogerim own the intake side; Jonas owns **ongoing client-animal medicine**.

## Ecosystem role

Jonas validates multi-specialty scheduling, completed versus future visits, and the link between clinical outcomes and retail — without returns, death, or partner return events attached to his name.
