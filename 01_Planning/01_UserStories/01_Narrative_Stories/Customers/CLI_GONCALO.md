# Gonçalo Rego

Gonçalo Rego is the clinic’s most visible **anchor client** in the first launch weeks: he connects shelter intake, adoption, repeat shopping, and ongoing veterinary care for two household animals. He registered on **6 May 2026**, after the shelter corridor had already begun to fill, and has remained an active portal user since.

## Who he is in the ecosystem

Gonçalo lives in the Braga area and uses the client portal with a Portuguese interface and dark theme preference. Ivo Sá created his account during the onboarding burst that followed the core team’s arrival. Before he had credentials, he had already met the front desk informally through Isabel Carvalho’s early presence at the clinic.

His animals are **Rogerim**, adopted from the Patinhas do Ave transfer on **8 May**, and **Jonas**, a cat who was already under Gonçalo’s ownership before the May launch window. Together they make Gonçalo the reference household for “shelter animal becomes family member” and for repeat visits across more than one clinical specialty.

## How his story unfolds

Rogerim’s path begins on **4 May**, when Tiago Mendes receives the dog from Associação Patinhas do Ave as an internal shelter animal. Gonçalo registers two days later; on **8 May** Tiago completes the adoption that opens ownership for Rogerim. The interval between intake and adoption is deliberate in the simulation: it mirrors a short waiting period while the client account and visit planning catch up.

Jonas follows a steadier clinical rhythm with João Marcelo. A general consultation completes on **12 May**; dermatology on **16 May** leads to a diet change reflected in a shop purchase on **18 May**, handled by João Navarro at the commercial desk. A further dermatology appointment remains **scheduled** for **10 June**, with a reminder issued on **9 June** as part of the clinic’s morning notification routine.

Retail visits on **10 May** (food and toys) and **18 May** (nutrition linked to Jonas’s care) show Gonçalo as a repeat buyer, not a one-time adopter. Marta Ribeiro crosses his path only in passing — they share the waiting room on **17 May** when Marta arrives late for Felix’s visit — but the narrative does not tie them beyond that ambient contact.

## Friction and trust

On **23 May**, Gonçalo generates a cluster of failed sign-in attempts caused by repeated typos in his email address. The pattern is documented for audit and training purposes; occasional password mistakes appear as well. These episodes do not change his operational status as an active client; they illustrate how authentication history accumulates around a otherwise cooperative household.

## Relationships that matter

Tiago Mendes owns the shelter-facing steps for Rogerim. João Marcelo is Jonas’s clinician across general and dermatology work. João Navarro links consultations to invoices and products. Isabel bridges desk greeting and scheduling habits before and after her own return from absence. Ivo remains the administrator who registered the account and oversees platform configuration.

Gonçalo’s arc validates the clinic’s public face: adoption from a partner shelter, multi-animal ownership, appointment lifecycles including a future scheduled visit, commercial follow-through, and reminder-driven communication — without relying on edge cases such as returns or account inactivation.
