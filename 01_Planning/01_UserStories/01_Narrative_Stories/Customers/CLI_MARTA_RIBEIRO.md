# Marta Ribeiro

Marta Ribeiro represents the **commercial and scheduling stress** side of the launch window: she buys, returns, arrives late, cancels, and leaves an invoice unpaid. She registered on **8 May 2026** at Isabel Carvalho’s desk with Ivo Sá creating the account, and remains active in the system through the simulation end. Her relationship with the clinic is more uneven than Gonçalo Rego’s steady household arc.

## Who she is in the ecosystem

Marta uses the client portal with Portuguese language settings and a light theme. Her primary animal is **Felix**, a young dog adopted on **8 May** from a local shelter mix (not a Patinhas transfer). Clinical work runs through João Marcelo with an acute visit spanning **13–14 May** and a follow-up on **17 May** after she arrives late. Felix is the reason Marta appears in waiting-room and notification scenarios alongside other clients.

She is not a shelter adopter in the documented cast; her value to the simulation is retail friction, appointment discipline, and accounts receivable behaviour after the first month of operations.

## How her story unfolds

On **9 May**, Marta completes a purchase at the desk with João Navarro — kibble and a lead — establishing her as a retail customer before the more difficult events. After Felix’s **13–14 May** visit (which runs from in progress to completed with Marcelo delayed), Navarro invoices pharmacy items on **14 May**; that invoice line later feeds the overdue story.

**17 May** is the turning point for scheduling reputation. Marta arrives **late** for Felix’s follow-up consultation after traffic on the A3. The visit still completes, but the lateness is recorded as part of the operational discipline layer. Gonçalo Rego is present in the same waiting-room moment, which ties two unrelated households in a single ambient chronology entry without merging their accounts.

**18 May** brings a **return** of a defective toy from an earlier purchase, processed again by João Navarro — a credit-note style commercial event distinct from the pharmacy pending invoice.

**24 May**, Marta **cancels** a surgery-prep appointment for a June slot. The cancellation is explicit in the timeline — it contrasts with Gonçalo’s forward-looking scheduled visit in June. Unlike Pedro Costa, Marta communicates; she is documented as not no-showing.

By **5 June**, the pending invoice from **14 May** is **overdue**. The clinic therefore carries both a progressing clinical path for Felix and an open receivable on the commercial side.

## Relationships that matter

João Navarro is her commercial counterpart for purchase and return. João Marcelo owns Felix’s clinical narrative. Isabel Carvalho and the broader desk team appear in scheduling and waiting-room context. Ivo Sá remains the administrator backdrop for account creation and platform configuration.

Marta’s story does not include adoption, shelter transfer, or account inactivation. It complements Gonçalo’s anchor household by showing how a single client can accumulate **multiple operational signals** — lateness, cancellation, return, overdue payment — while staying active.
