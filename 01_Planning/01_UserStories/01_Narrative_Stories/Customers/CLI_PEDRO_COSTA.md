# Pedro Costa

Pedro Costa is the **discipline and inactivation** client in the launch cast: a missed appointment, a dog who remains tied to the account, and a move to inactive status after the first month of operations.

## Who he is in the ecosystem

Pedro registered on **9 May 2026**. His portal profile follows the same Portuguese, Braga-area pattern as other clients in the simulation. His animal **Thor** is named in the operational customer record even though Thor does not have a separate animal dossier in the Animals folder — the household is still understood as “client plus dog” for scheduling and no-show behaviour.

Pedro is not a shelter adopter in the documented timeline and does not drive retail returns or overdue invoices in the same way as Marta Ribeiro.

## How his story unfolds

On **15 May**, Pedro **does not attend** a scheduled appointment. The no-show is a first-class operational event: it exists to exercise absence handling, client reputation signals, and follow-up posture without inventing a second missed visit or a complex clinical history.

After that sharp failure, Pedro **cancels** a second-chance appointment on **22 May** (more than twenty-four hours ahead). Isabel notes the earlier no-call-no-show in internal commentary; Marcelo’s slot is freed without a no-show repeat.

On **2 June**, Pedro’s client status becomes **inactive** — Ivo records a move abroad. Thor remains on the registry under the inactive client as a demo edge for reporting. Authentication attempts on an inactive account may still appear in the operational layer.

Pedro does not adopt from Patinhas, does not complete the multi-visit arcs of Gonçalo or Ana, and does not generate Gonçalo’s authentication typo cluster. His narrative is narrow: no-show, cancelled retry, then inactive client with animal still linked.

## Relationships that matter

Desk and clinical staff appear only as the generic clinic roles implied by the missed appointment — Marcelo’s calendar load includes other animals; Pedro’s no-show is not expanded into a rescheduled chain in the provided material. Tiago’s shelter work does not intersect Pedro’s household in the documented events.

Pedro exists so the ecosystem documentation can speak about **failed attendance** and **inactive clients** in human terms, alongside happier adoption stories and Marta’s commercial noise.
